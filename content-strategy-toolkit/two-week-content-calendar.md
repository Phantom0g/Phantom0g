# Two-Week Content Calendar: Talent Competition

**Status:** Illustrative campaign concept; not a record of a delivered campaign.  
**Primary objective:** Qualified contestant registrations.  
**Secondary objective:** Audience participation and event awareness.  
**Audience:** Emerging talents and their supporters in Nigeria.  
**Primary channel:** X; adapt selected assets for Instagram or TikTok.

Replace Day 1–14 with dates. Insert confirmed venue, event date, entry rules and a working registration link before use.

| Day | Theme | Post concept / format | Call to action | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Launch | Announcement graphic plus a short explanation of who can enter | Read the rules and register | Registration-link clicks |
| 2 | Explain | Thread answering eligibility and entry questions | Read the entry guide | Link clicks; repeated questions |
| 3 | Spotlight | Approved contestant introduction with a short performance clip | Support the contestant in replies | Relevant replies; shares |
| 4 | Conversation | Ask supporters what makes a performance memorable | Reply with one quality | Relevant replies |
| 5 | Trust | Introduce a confirmed judge or team member | Read their role in the event | Profile visits; link clicks |
| 6 | Behind the scenes | Rehearsal or planning clip with a factual caption | Follow for the next update | Video views; new followers |
| 7 | Weekly recap | Recap key announcements and answer outstanding questions | Share with an eligible talent | Shares; completed registrations |
| 8 | Spotlight | Showcase another approved contestant or talent category | Join the conversation | Relevant replies; shares |
| 9 | Practical help | Registration checklist graphic | Complete the registration steps | Completed registrations |
| 10 | Community | Ask which talent categories supporters want to see | Reply with a category | Relevant replies |
| 11 | Partner feature | Explain a confirmed partner's contribution | Explore the partner's role | Partner-link clicks |
| 12 | Deadline reminder | State the actual registration deadline and remaining steps | Register before the deadline | Completed registrations |
| 13 | Event guide | Confirmed venue, time, access and attendance information | Save and share the guide | Shares; information-link clicks |
| 14 | Final recap | Bring together registration, attendance and support links | Take the relevant next step | Registrations; attendance interest |

## Daily operating checklist
- Check new replies, mentions and enquiries.
- Answer using confirmed event information.
- Direct registration questions to the working entry page.
- Record repeated questions for the next FAQ.
- Log the post URL, publication time and results at a consistent interval.

## Week-one review
Compare posts against the primary objective. High views alone do not establish registration success. Record which themes drove link clicks and verified registrations, then adjust week two.
