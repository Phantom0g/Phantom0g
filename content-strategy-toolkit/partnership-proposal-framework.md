# Event Venue Partnership Proposal Framework

**Status:** Reusable template. Replace every bracketed field before sending.

## 1. The opportunity
[Organizer] proposes a themed event series at [venue]. The intended audience is [audience], with [confirmed or explicitly estimated attendance] guests per event.

The concept combines [entertainment] and [food offering] with the venue's [drinks / facilities / service].

## 2. Event concept
| Item | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | [theme and why it fits the venue] |
| Date and hours | [proposed dates and operating hours] |
| Attendance | [capacity and the basis of any forecast] |
| Entry | [ticketing model and proposed price, if applicable] |
| Entertainment | [confirmed acts or booking plan] |
| Food | [supplier and service plan] |

## 3. Responsibilities
| Organizer | Venue |
| --- | --- |
| [Entertainment and talent coordination] | [Approved space and agreed facilities] |
| [Food arrangements] | [Drinks inventory and service] |
| [Ticketing and guest communications] | [Agreed staffing and venue operations] |
| [Promotional assets and campaign execution] | [Agreed promotional support] |

Allocate security, cleaning, sound, utilities, access control and incident handling explicitly.

## 4. Commercial terms to agree
- **Ticket income:** [who collects it, allocation and settlement timing].
- **Drink sales revenue share:** [proposed percentage] of [clearly defined eligible drink sales].
- **Sales calculation:** [gross or net basis, taxes, discounts, refunds and exclusions].
- **Sales records:** [POS access or agreed daily sales report].
- **Settlement:** [date, payment method and reconciliation owner].
- **Costs:** [each party's costs and any minimum guarantee].
- **Cancellation:** [notice, refunds and treatment of committed costs].

A percentage alone is not a complete commercial arrangement; define what it applies to and how it will be verified.

## 5. Promotion and measurement
[Campaign channels, approved branding, publishing dates and responsibilities.]

Track ticket sales, attendance, eligible drink sales and guest feedback. Mark forecasts as forecasts rather than confirmed outcomes.

## 6. Proposed next step
Arrange a venue walkthrough and commercial discussion to confirm dates, responsibilities and terms.
