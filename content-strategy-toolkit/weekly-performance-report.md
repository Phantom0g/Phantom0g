## 2. Metric definitions
- **Net follower change:** ending followers minus starting followers.
- **Follower growth (%):** net change divided by starting followers, multiplied by 100. If the starting value is zero, report N/A.
- **Engagement rate by impressions (%):** included engagements divided by impressions, multiplied by 100. State which engagements are included; report N/A for zero impressions.
- **Conversion rate (%):** verified conversions divided by the explicitly named denominator, multiplied by 100. State whether that denominator is clicks, visits or enquiries.
- **Cost per result:** relevant ad spend divided by verified results. Report N/A when there are zero results.
- Do not add platform reach totals and label the sum as unique people: the same person may be counted on multiple platforms.
