# The Big Bhang: Audience Growth & Event Partnership Outreach

**Role:** Twitter/X Manager; project coordination and proposal writing  
**Sector:** Entertainment / talent competition  
**Location:** Nigeria  
**Focus:** Audience participation, contestant visibility, partnership outreach and event promotion.

## Problem
The competition needed visibility, active audience participation and brand relationships. Promotion had to connect contestants, supporters and potential partners around the event.

## Action
### 1. Build an active conversation on X
I managed content and audience interactions, encouraged support for contestants, and used conversation, tags and hashtags to keep the competition visible.

### 2. Prepare partnership proposals
I developed and submitted pitches to brands and entertainment stakeholders, supported follow-up and communicated the opportunity to prospective partners.

### 3. Coordinate promotion around the event
I worked across social media and project activities to support contestant participation, partnership outreach and the event's promotional needs.

## Result
| Measure | Reported outcome | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Follower growth | Approximately 70% | Reported without paid ads |
| Contestants | 200+ | Event participation; a team/event outcome |
| Event attendees | Approximately 500 | Approximate reported attendance |
| Proposals sent | Approximately 40 | Outreach volume |
| Positive partnership responses | Approximately 34 | Positive responses, not necessarily signed or fulfilled agreements |

## My contribution
My work centred on X management, audience engagement, proposal preparation, outreach and project coordination. Contestant and attendance totals describe the event, rather than outcomes attributable solely to my social media work.
