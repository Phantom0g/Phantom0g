# Expedia Consult: Building a Structured Social Media Presence

**Role:** Social Media Manager / Business Developer  
**Sector:** Travel services  
**Location:** Ikeja, Lagos, Nigeria  
**Scope:** Instagram, TikTok and Facebook; content coordination, advertising support, customer engagement and reporting.

## Problem
The brand needed a more consistent marketing approach. Challenges included low engagement and enquiries, weak branding, limited reporting and customer service gaps. Social media activity needed clearer campaign priorities and follow-through.

## Action
### 1. Establish a repeatable content workflow
I planned campaigns and content calendars around the agency's services, coordinated graphics and video production, and worked with a content creator and a videographer.

### 2. Improve brand communication
I developed copy and campaign ideas, improved visual consistency, and made service information clearer for prospective travellers.

### 3. Support enquiry handling and acquisition
I managed customer interactions and direct messages, supported Meta and TikTok advertising, and helped align promotional activity with business development.

### 4. Make performance visible
I introduced weekly and monthly reporting to document activity and outcomes and inform subsequent campaigns. I also worked on partnership proposals.

## Result
| Outcome | Reported result | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Combined social audience | Approximately 1,000 to over 6,000 followers | Aggregate across platforms; not a single-platform total |
| Customer enquiries | Increase reported | No verified count or conversion rate supplied |
| Brand presentation | Improved consistency | Qualitative outcome |
| Reporting | Regular performance reporting established | A process improvement |

## My contribution
I handled social media strategy and day-to-day management, campaign planning, copywriting, customer engagement, advertising coordination and reporting. Production involved collaboration with other team members.
