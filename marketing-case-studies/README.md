## How to read the results
- Expedia's follower total covers multiple platforms.
- Big Bhang's follower growth refers to my reported social media outcome; no claim is made about a paid advertising campaign.
- Positive partnership responses are separate from signed agreements or delivered sponsorships.
- Audience growth and enquiries do not establish revenue or conversion improvements without separate sales records.
